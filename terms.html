Terms of Use & Attribution Policy

Please read these guidelines carefully before using, sharing, or referencing any materials from Class Resources.

1. Purpose & Educational Intent

Overview
  • Class Resources is an independent, non-profit academic initiative created and maintained by Amitendu Bikash Dhusiya.
  • The repository hosts BCA syllabus copies, lecture notes, continuous assessment submissions (CA1/CA2), exam presentations, and question papers intended exclusively for educational study and exam preparation.

2. What You Can Do (Permitted Use)

Permissions
  • Personal Study & Reference: You are free to view, download, read, and print any files for your individual learning, homework assistance, revision, and examination preparations.
  • Sharing with Classmates: You may share individual files or direct website links with college peers, student study circles, and teachers.
  • Contributing Content: You are welcomed and encouraged to suggest corrections, add missing question papers, or submit notes for any BCA semester via our GitHub repository.

4. Restrictions & Commercial Prohibition

Restrictions

5. No Guarantees & Content Availability

Disclaimer
