# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing an open, welcoming, and inclusive community for all students, educators, and contributors to Class Resources, regardless of background, identity, skill level, or university affiliation.

## Expected Behavior
All community members, whether submitting study resources, opening pull requests, or commenting on issues, are expected to:
- Be respectful, courteous, and constructive in all discussions.
- Welcome newcomers and encourage peer learning.
- Respect academic integrity: clearly attribute sources where applicable and avoid submitting confidential university internal documents.
- Provide thoughtful feedback when reviewing contributions or reporting errors in course notes.

## Unacceptable Behavior
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across this repository and its related forums:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, insults, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, disruptive commentary, or spamming unsolicited links.
- Removing or obfuscating original creator attribution.
- Uploading malicious files, harmful links, or copyrighted material outside academic fair use.
